02 —Possible bids · Go / No-Go

Every possible bid lands here first. Rate the six questions Good, OK or Poor and the score suggests Go or No-Go. Territory and Size & bonding are gates: Poor on either suggests No-Go. Your call always wins. Go puts the job on the bid schedule; No-Go logs it as Passed with the reason.

Undecided bids show dashed on the Bid Board Schedule calendar, where the Undecided filter turns them off.

    One button keeps the board current. It reads the Current Projects folder with your Microsoft 365 connection, adds any new job folder dated today or later, then goes into every open bid's folder.
